Questions that guide my work

Research

How do cultural narratives shape what people believe is possible—and how do institutions reinforce or challenge those beliefs?

My interests lie at the intersection of culture, inequality, organizations, and economic mobility. I am especially interested in beliefs about merit, deservingness, and belonging, and in how these beliefs inform judgments about who receives opportunity and investment.

Areas of inquiry

Cultural narratives and socialization

How beliefs about merit, possibility, and worth are formed and internalized.

Institutions and evaluation

How organizational practices and individual judgments shape access to resources and opportunities.

Inequality and mobility

How cultural and structural conditions interact across life trajectories.
