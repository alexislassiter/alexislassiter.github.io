A little about me

About

I’m a researcher and strategist interested in how people and institutions understand opportunity, belonging, and mobility.

My work spans research, education policy, organizational strategy, and leadership development. Across these experiences, I’ve become increasingly interested in how the stories we tell about success and deservingness shape individual choices and institutional decisions.

Background

I hold an Ed.M. in Education Policy and Analysis from Harvard University and a B.S. in Economics from Winston-Salem State University.
